    Storage & containers

    Steam's public inventory can't see inside Storage Units, so record what each one holds here. When you move items in or out in the game, the next Steam sync notices them leaving or coming back and suggests the change for you to confirm. Stored items count towards your Total Portfolio and are listed there too. Each container holds up to 1,000 items.

    Profit & loss

    What you've spent and made on items you bought and sold. Prices are up to you: each figure uses the items that have the prices it needs. Entries come from the Containers tab (items a Steam sync noticed leaving or arriving), from items you add with a purchase price, from items you remove and say you sold, and from anything you record here.

    Listing guide by market

    How it chooses: items whose after-fee price has been falling go first and rising ones are kept; copies from big stacks go before one-offs; expensive single items are kept longest. Each market is used best price first, but only for about as many as it sells in 30 days, so later copies may go elsewhere for less. Cheap copies cover the last few pounds rather than selling something expensive that overshoots.

    Exchange rates

    Prices are the lowest current listing on each market, converted to your base currency with daily exchange rates. "Cheapest" is the best place to buy right now among the markets you've selected. Market fees and withdrawal limits differ between sites and are not included. Click any column heading to sort; click again to reverse, and a third time to go back to the normal order.

    Prices: Steam Community Market, Skinport, CSFloat, Market.CSGO and Waxpeer public APIs. Exchange rates: Frankfurter (ECB reference rates), fallback Rates by Exchange Rate API.

    Applied stickers, patches & charms

    Applied stickers add only a share of their cheapest loose price (5–15 % by default, reduced by scrape), and the total is capped relative to the skin's own price. Adjust both in Settings.

    Add manual price quote

    Record a price you have seen on another marketplace (for example Buff163 in CNY). It is converted to your base currency with today's exchange rate and included in comparisons and valuation.
